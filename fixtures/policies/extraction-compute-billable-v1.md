# Service terms: structured extraction, compute-billable

Policy ID: extraction-compute-billable-v1
Applies to: asynchronous structured-extraction jobs purchased per operation.

1. Delivery. The obligation is met when a completed result artifact for the operation is stored and can be retrieved by the buyer who paid for it.

2. Billable compute. The job price pays for compute that has started. If the job fails after compute has started, the price remains billable and no repayment is due. If the job fails before any compute starts, the seller permits a full repayment of the principal job price; fees are not repaid.

3. Timeouts. A timeout, by itself, is not a terminal failure.

4. Evidence. Claims of completion or failure must be supported by the seller's service records or the stored artifact. A buyer's statement is a claim, not proof.

5. Changes. These terms apply as written when the order is placed. Later versions do not change an existing order.
