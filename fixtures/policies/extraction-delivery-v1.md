# Service terms: structured extraction, delivery-based

Policy ID: extraction-delivery-v1
Applies to: asynchronous structured-extraction jobs purchased per operation.

1. Delivery. The obligation is met when a completed result artifact for the operation is stored and can be retrieved by the buyer who paid for it. A response lost in transit does not void delivery if the stored artifact remains retrievable.

2. Refundable failure. If the job reaches a terminal failure before producing a usable artifact, the seller permits a full repayment of the job price. A full repayment is the principal job price stated in the order. Protocol, network, gas, and service fees are not repaid.

3. Timeouts. A timeout, by itself, is not a terminal failure. A job may still be running or may have completed after the requester stopped waiting.

4. Evidence. Claims of completion or failure must be supported by the seller's service records or the stored artifact. A buyer's statement is a claim, not proof.

5. Changes. These terms apply as written when the order is placed. Later versions do not change an existing order.
